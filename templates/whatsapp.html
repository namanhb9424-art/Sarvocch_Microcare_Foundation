Meta blue tick (Official Business Account)

The blue/green tick is awarded by Meta after it reviews your business - it can't be built into the app. This prototype shows the checklist and tracks progress.

  • Create a Meta Business account (business.facebook.com)
  • Complete Meta Business Verification with legal documents
  • Set up WhatsApp Business Platform with a dedicated number
  • Get the display name approved
  • Apply for the official tick (Meta decides eligibility)

Sending automated messages through the official WhatsApp Business Platform later will need Meta's API (it may involve conversation charges), so it's left out of this free prototype. Please confirm current requirements with Meta.
